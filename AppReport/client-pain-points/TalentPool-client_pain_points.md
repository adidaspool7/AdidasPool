# Talent Pool — Client Pain Points & Survey Questions

**Data:** 29 de abril de 2026

---

## Pain Points identified from client meetings

1. **Organize candidate information** — they use Excel sometimes
2. **Know which candidates applied before or are new** — because of GDPR, they can keep candidate info for only 6 months
3. **Screening for language skills** (extendable to any skill) — not to replace current process, but a first screening layer would be valuable
4. **Direct contact with candidates** — related to notifications and campaigns features
5. **Save time on analysing CVs and extracting info** — to see something as simple as "location", they have to open all CVs one by one
6. **Analytics** — understand what is going on with the recruitment process with organized and clear views
7. **Understand where to focus recruitment efforts** — e.g. target German speakers, internship candidates, tech profiles, etc.
8. **Empower HR productivity** — a tool that makes work feel better and more structured
9. **Keep engagement of candidates not selected** — related to the 6-month GDPR rule; keep showing interest via assessments, campaigns, and improvement tracks ("borderline" candidates)

> **Note:** This talent pool project is for HR teams / recruiters. The "Onboarding Assistant" is the separate product targeting employees/workers.

## Survey Question Bank

One question per pain point, with backup options where relevant. All questions are phrased neutrally — they describe the problem without hinting at any solution.

---

### Pain point 1 — Organizing candidate information

> **"What tool does your team primarily use to store and manage candidate information during a recruitment process?"**
> *(Excel / Google Sheets / An ATS (e.g. Workday, Greenhouse) / Email folders / Shared documents / No structured system)*

**Why:** If "Excel" or "no structured system" dominates, it is direct evidence for the talent pool database.

---

### Pain point 2 — Knowing if a candidate has applied before (GDPR / 6-month rule)

> **"How does your team currently identify whether a candidate has previously applied for a position at adidas Porto?"**
> *(We check manually in our records / We rely on the candidate declaring it / We have no way to verify this / We use an automated system)*

**Backup:**
> **"Has your team ever lost useful information about a past candidate because data retention policies required it to be deleted?"**
> *(Yes, frequently / Yes, occasionally / Rarely / Never / Not sure)*

**Why:** These two together expose both the GDPR data loss problem and the absence of a structured re-identification system.

---

### Pain point 3 — Language / skills screening

> **"At what stage of your recruitment process do you currently perform a first formal check of a candidate's language proficiency or technical skills?"**
> *(Before the first interview / During the first interview / Only in a dedicated assessment round / We don't perform a formal check — we rely on the CV)*

**Why:** "We rely on the CV" or "no formal check" validates the need for an early screening layer — and extends naturally to any skill type, not just language.

---

### Pain point 4 — Direct contact and communication with candidates

> **"How does your team currently keep candidates informed about their application status or upcoming opportunities?"**
> *(Individual emails written manually / Automated emails via an ATS / Phone calls / We don't have a structured process)*

**Backup:**
> **"Has your team ever had to re-engage a candidate for a new position after they were not selected for a previous one? If yes, how did you manage that outreach?"**
> *(Yes, and it was easy / Yes, but it was difficult to find their information / No, we don't reuse past candidates / Not applicable)*

---

### Pain point 5 — Saving time on CV analysis

> **"When screening a batch of CVs, how long does it typically take your team to extract key information (e.g. location, years of experience, languages) from each individual CV?"**
> *(Under 1 minute / 1–3 minutes / 3–5 minutes / More than 5 minutes)*

**Why:** Simple, quantifiable. Multiply by average batch size and you get a striking total time figure for your report.

**Backup:**
> **"How often do you open a CV only to find immediately that the candidate does not meet a basic requirement (e.g. wrong location, missing language)?"**
> *(Almost always / Often / Sometimes / Rarely)*

---

### Pain point 6 — Analytics and visibility over the recruitment process

> **"How easy is it for you to get a quick overview of your current recruitment pipeline — for example, how many candidates are at each stage, or how many applications were received per job opening?"**
> *(Very easy — I have a dashboard / Possible but takes time to compile / Very difficult / I have no visibility)*

---

### Pain point 7 — Understanding where to focus recruitment efforts

> **"When planning a new recruitment campaign, how does your team identify which skills, languages, or profiles are most in demand across the business?"**
> *(We analyse data from past recruitments / We rely on input from managers / It is done intuitively / There is no structured process for this)*

**Why:** "No structured process" or "done intuitively" validates the analytics + job-matching engine as a strategic planning tool.

---

### Pain point 8 — HR tool that empowers productivity

> **"On a scale of 1 to 5, how would you rate the degree to which your current recruitment tools help you work more efficiently and reduce repetitive manual tasks?"**
> *(1 = They add very little value / 5 = They significantly boost my productivity)*

**Why:** A low average score here is a strong headline figure for your report — it directly motivates the whole platform.

---

### Pain point 9 — Keeping borderline candidates engaged (GDPR + re-engagement)

> **"What happens to candidates who were not selected for a position but showed potential — for example, strong language skills or relevant experience?"**
> *(We keep their profile for future openings / We inform them of other opportunities proactively / They are rejected and removed from our records / We have no structured process for this group)*

**Why:** This is the most differentiated pain point of the platform. If most say "rejected and removed" or "no structured process", it proves the need for the improvement track + campaign engagement system.
