\chapter{API Reference}
\label{app:api}

This appendix lists the complete REST surface of the platform. All routes are
file-based Next.js App Router handlers under \texttt{src/app/api/}; each maps
\texttt{[METHOD] /api/[resource]} to a single use-case method. Responses are
JSON (except the CSV export), request bodies are validated with Zod, and
errors follow the convention \texttt{\{ error: "message" \}}. Authorisation is
enforced at the middleware layer described in
\secref{sec:design-authz}: every route requires an authenticated
Supabase session except the public prefixes, and the HR-only subset
additionally requires \texttt{app\_metadata.role === "hr"}.

The \emph{Access} column summarises the effective authorisation: \emph{Public}
routes are listed in the middleware \texttt{PUBLIC\_API\_PREFIXES}; \emph{Auth}
routes require any authenticated session; \emph{HR} routes require the
\texttt{hr} role (enforced either by \texttt{HR\_ONLY\_API\_PREFIXES} or by the
inline \texttt{requireHr()} helper); and \emph{Candidate} routes are scoped to
the signed-in candidate's own data.
